# Jobs to Be Done

Why people use the Bill Splitting Calculator. Each job is something the person needs to get done whether or not this app exists, so no job mentions the app, its screens or its buttons. Each job names the role it belongs to (see [app-roles.md](app-roles.md)), and every story in [user-stories.md](user-stories.md) names the job it serves.

## Solo diner

**J1. Know exactly what I owe**
When I've finished a meal or a purchase on my own and the bill adds tax, extra fees and a tip, I want to know the exact amount to pay, to the cent, so I can pay the right amount without doing the maths in my head.
- Role: Solo diner

**J2. Keep a record of my own spending**
When I look back at what I've spent on meals and purchases, I want an accurate record of each past bill that I can correct or remove, so I can keep track of my spending and trust the numbers.
- Role: Solo diner

## Group member

**J3. Share a bill evenly**
When a group of us shares one bill and we agree to split it evenly, I want each person's share worked out so that the shares add up to exactly the bill, so I can make sure nobody pays a cent too much or too little.
- Role: Group member

**J4. Pay for what each person had**
When a group of us orders different things, including dishes some of us share, I want each person to pay for what they had plus a fair part of the tax, fees and tip, so I can make the split fair to people who ordered less.
- Role: Group member

**J5. Charge only the people who were there**
When one friend pays for an outing that only some of our friend group joined, I want to note who paid and who took part, so I can make sure only the people who were there owe a share and the person who paid gets it back.
- Role: Group member

**J6. Know who owes whom overall**
When my friends and I share costs again and again over weeks or months, I want to know at any moment what each person owes or is owed in total, and the fewest payments that would square everyone up, so I can settle debts without long lists of back-and-forth payments.
- Role: Group member

**J7. Count repayments**
When one friend pays another back, I want that repayment taken off what they owed, even if they pay back more than they owed, so I can keep everyone's running totals correct.
- Role: Group member

**J8. Include friends who don't keep records**
When some friends in our circle don't keep their own records of shared costs, I want to include them in our shared costs from the start and let them take over their own part whenever they choose to, so I can keep everyone's history complete.
- Role: Group member

**J9. See who changed what**
When anyone in our group adds, changes or removes a shared cost or a repayment, I want to see who did it, when, and what changed, so I can trust the totals and sort out disagreements.
- Role: Group member

**J10. Keep history right when people leave or come back**
When someone leaves our circle of friends, or comes back later, I want their past share to stay exactly as it was and their access to our shared records to end or return with them, so I can keep our history correct and keep it private to the people currently in the group.
- Role: Group member

**J11. Close out a group for good**
When I'm the last person still keeping a group's shared records and we're finished with them, I want to close them out completely, so I can leave nothing behind.
- Role: Group member

## Solo diner and Group member

**J12. Keep my money records private**
When I keep records of what I've spent, paid or am owed, I want only me, or only the friends a record concerns, to be able to see or change them, so I can trust that nobody else reads or alters my financial details.
- Roles: Solo diner, Group member

**J13. Get back in without help**
When I forget the secret I use to protect my records, I want a way to regain access that doesn't depend on anyone else, so I can make sure I never lose my history.
- Roles: Solo diner, Group member

**J14. Catch mistakes before relying on a figure**
When I'm working out money amounts and I make a typing mistake or enter something impossible, I want the mistake caught and clearly explained before I rely on the result, so I can never pay or charge someone the wrong amount.
- Roles: Solo diner, Group member

**J15. See amounts in my own currency**
When I pay in a particular currency, I want amounts shown with that currency's symbol and kept in one currency throughout, so I can read every figure without confusion.
- Roles: Solo diner, Group member

**J16. Use the browser I already have**
When I need to work out or check a bill, I want to do it in whichever common, up-to-date web browser I already use, so I can avoid installing or buying anything extra.
- Roles: Solo diner, Group member

**J20. See my overall position at a glance**
When I have money spent on my own and money owed between me and several groups of friends, I want one overview of what I owe and what I'm owed, so I can know where I stand without adding things up across places.
- Roles: Solo diner, Group member

**J21. Read figures comfortably in any light**
When I check money figures in a bright room or a dim restaurant at night, I want the display to suit the lighting and my own preference, so I can read amounts without strain.
- Roles: Solo diner, Group member

**J22. Manage money without a mouse or perfect eyesight**
When I rely on a keyboard, software that reads text aloud, large text or high contrast, I want to do everything anyone else can do with my money records, so I can manage bills on my own without asking for help.
- Roles: Solo diner, Group member

## Host

**J17. Try it on my own computer for free**
When I want to try out or review a piece of software, I want to install and run my own copy on my computer with only free tools and no outside accounts or services, so I can evaluate it without paying or signing up for anything.
- Role: Host

**J18. Run it on my own server**
When I want my friends to use a tool for real, I want to run it on a server I control, without depending on any third-party company's services, so I can keep it running and keep our data with us.
- Role: Host

**J19. Get proof that it works**
When I evaluate a piece of software, I want quick, repeatable proof that it behaves as described in the common web browsers, so I can trust its claims without checking everything by hand.
- Role: Host
